Hexadecimal Explorer

One hexadecimal value, two ways to interpret data: ASCII text and RGB color.

Enter 2, 4, or 6 digits for 1–3 ASCII characters. Enter exactly 6 digits for a color. Click ASCII to reveal text or Color to reveal the color. You can click both without resetting.
